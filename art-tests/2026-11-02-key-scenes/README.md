# Key scenes (Art Style Proofs, Round 11)

Eight scenes from the Notion design space, one take each on `gemini-3-pro-image` at 1K, no cherry-picking. Viewable with verdicts in the Art Style Proofs artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/KRSZv6wmMPtb43g2FbdocX).

- `prompts.json`: the scene prompts. Each request was the scene prompt, then `Style:` and the locked barbarian style prompt, then the Round 8 framing line, plus the scene rule for locations.
- `log.json`: per-image result and seconds.
- `*.webp`: the images. The emperor and the skin witch came with a cream page border, trimmed and re-cropped to their aspect ratio.
