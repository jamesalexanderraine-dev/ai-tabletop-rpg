# Key scenes, rewritten prompts (Art Style Proofs, Round 12)

The eight Round 11 scenes (`../2026-11-02-key-scenes`) with each scene prompt rewritten against its verdict. Two takes each on `gemini-3-pro-image` at 1K, no cherry-picking. The style prompt and framing line are unchanged from Round 8. Viewable with before/after and verdicts in the Art Style Proofs artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/KRSZv6wmMPtb43g2FbdocX).

- `prompts.json`: the rewritten scene prompts.
- `log.json`: per-image result and seconds.
- `<scene>-v2-<take>.webp`: the images. Borders trimmed on joust 2, great hall 2 and skin witch 1 (large), plateau 1 and rooted city 1 (a few pixels).

What moved the needle: lead with the action ("Action shot", "Comedy scene"), name what not to draw ("no castles", "no pentagram", "not floating"), and describe materials instead of naming an architect (honeycomb, candle wax and stone fruit got Gaudí; "Sagrada Família" alone got vines).
