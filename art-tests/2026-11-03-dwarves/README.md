# Dwarven mountains (Art Style Proofs, Round 16)

The sunward face of the dwarven mountains, two takes on `gemini-3-pro-image`, built from the new `dwarves` region and `dwarf-face` location sheets in `src/art/visual-bible.json` (`scenes.json`; full prompt in `log.json`).

- Take 1 has the canon gate (cantilevered slabs over the falls) but boxy homes; take 2 has Wright-style homes with planted roofs but a weak gate.
- Next try: homes "long, low horizontal houses with bands of windows, in the same style as the gate"; the gate as "one great building".

## Refined (scenes-v2.json, log-v2.json, face-v2-*.webp)

Homes rewritten as Habitat 67 (James's suggestion): stacked, offset stone blocks, a garden on every roof, walkways and stairs between; the gate as "ONE great building" in the same style. Refined take 1 gets both homes and gate right in one image; take 2 drifts toward an Egyptian/Aztec temple gate.

## Harbour and life (scenes-v3.json, log-v3.json, face-v3-*.webp, harbour-*.webp)

James asked for a trade harbour where the water meets the mountains, food in the rooftop gardens and signs of life. Built as a river port (canon: trade hub, metalwork floating down to the delta). Face take 2 is the best dwarf image yet (food gardens with dwarves tending them, a true Fallingwater gate, a working harbour); take 1 came in too close and gave the gate a castle door. Both harbour close-ups work. Thin edge trimmed on face take 2.

## Busier harbour (scenes-v4.json, log-v4.json, face-v4-*.webp, harbour-v2-*.webp)

Harbour sheet rewritten for crowds (barges three deep, a row of cranes, warehouses in the cliff base, quay rail carts, streams of workers and traders); the landscape pins the harbour to the lower third. Face take 1 is the most complete dwarf image yet; take 2's homes slid back to boxy cubes. Harbour take 2 is the busiest (black border trimmed); take 1 printed "ARMOR"/"STEEL" on crates.
